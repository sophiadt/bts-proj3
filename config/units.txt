UNIT SCALE — lab biomechanical facial model
Confirmed: 2026-08-12

AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE
  Paper: "Beneath the Skin: Interactive Biomechanical Facial Simulations
          via Composable Co-Expressions", Appendix C (Facial biomechanics).
          papers/Beneath the Skin ... .pdf
  Quote: "We measure the mandible of the virtual head model, and compare it
          with the measurement of the mandible of one of the authors of this
          survey.  In Maya, the width of the mandible is 155.92 units, and
          the author's counterpart is 15 cm.  Thus, the unit mapping is
          0.0962 cm per Maya unit."
  Also Table 2, row "Unit mapping in Maya": 0.0962 cm per Maya unit.

  So the scale is anthropometric, not a scene setting: 15 cm / 155.92 units
  = 0.096203 cm/unit.  It is one author's mandible, measured once -- treat
  0.962 as a stated constant of the model, not a measured property of a
  population.

THE NUMBERS
  1 Maya unit = 9.62e-4 m
              = 0.962 mm
              = 0.0962 cm

CONVERSIONS
  length  mm  = maya_units * 0.962
  area    mm2 = maya_units^2 * 0.925444
  volume  mm3 = maya_units^3 * 0.890277128     <-- USE THIS FOR VOLUME
