Capture Fit Follow-Up

A day of tests on the full fit of the 10 November burst capture, run through the Ziva rig. When the head says “not even you”, its mouth opens 46% as wide as the lab head’s. The pass mark is 70%. Nothing tried today moved it past 49%. The lip muscle is not the limit: it opens almost as far as the lab head’s, and the lips still don’t follow. Skin smoothing cut the folded triangles by about a fifth and left accuracy alone.

The gain is small. The wavy lower edge of the opening and the crack at the right corner stay in every run. The edge comes from the landmark contours, which the smoothing leaves alone on purpose. The corner artifacts come from the speech motion and also show on the lab head. The skin-only preview shows a mouth 83% as open, while the rig gives 46%, so the rig pictures are the true result.
